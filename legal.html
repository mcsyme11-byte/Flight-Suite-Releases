  • GitHub (api.github.com and GitHub Pages): only if you set up the always-on page, the share page, its snapshot and the landing videos you share (described in Section 5) are uploaded with your key to the public repository you chose, and GitHub stores and publishes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

Each of these services may receive your device’s IP address, as with any internet request, and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. The Developer does not control these services and is not responsible for their practices.

To record video clips and take screenshots, the Software controls OBS Studio on your computer through its local connection. To connect, it reads OBS’s own connection settings on your computer (it never changes them). Before a replay, it asks Squawk on your computer whether you are connected to VATSIM. Neither of these sends anything over the internet.

4. Data Security

Because your information is stored on your own computer, its security depends on the security of that computer. Keeping your operating system up to date and maintaining backups is recommended.

5. Sharing Your Flight

Share my flight is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the Software publishes a view-only page at your share link through Tailscale Funnel. The page shows only:

  • your callsign, aircraft type, and departure and arrival airports;
  • your live position, altitude, speed, heading, flight phase, estimated arrival time and distance to go;
  • your most recent landing (its rate, grade, crab angle at touchdown, airport, runway and time), during a flight as well as after it, and a summary of your most recent logged flight;
  • the landing videos you choose to share, each with its airport, runway, route, aircraft, landing rate, grade and date;
  • only if you turn on Show my career: your total time in the sim and in the air, distance flown, numbers of flights, airports, countries, aircraft types and flights on VATSIM, your best landing for each aircraft class and your recent landings, and, for each part you leave on, your last 30 days, your landing statistics, a map of the routes you have flown, your most flown aircraft and airports, your records and your milestones (landings with their date, route, aircraft, airport, runway, rate and grade), counting every flight in your logbook. A part of your career that you turn off is not sent to the page at all.

It never shows your name, your settings or any other information. The Software does not collect information about people who view the page; Tailscale delivers the page under its own privacy policy. When you turn Share my flight off, the page no longer shows your flight.

Landing videos. A landing video is shared only when you choose Share this landing on one of your clips. The Software makes a smaller copy of the clip (with the simulator’s sound only) for the page; the clip itself stays in your logbook. The page keeps your 20 most recently shared landings; older ones, and any you take down, are removed from the page but stay in your logbook.

The always-on page. If you set it up, a copy of the share page is kept on GitHub Pages so that it can be opened while your computer is off. After each logged flight, when you share or take down a landing video, and when you change these settings, the Software uploads a snapshot of the information above, other than your live position, and the shared landing videos to the public GitHub repository you chose, together with the address of your share link so that the copy can show your live flight while you fly. Each upload replaces everything before it, so the repository keeps no history of earlier snapshots or of videos you have taken down. Anyone can see the repository while it exists. When you turn Share my flight off, or remove the always-on page in Settings, the snapshot is replaced by a notice that you are not sharing; deleting the repository on GitHub removes everything.
