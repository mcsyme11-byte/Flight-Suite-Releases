What's in the suite.

Each app does one job and can be installed on its own. Playback and Hangar come with Logbook.

LogbookEvery flight logged by itself, with an accurate landing rate. PlaybackYour landing, flown again by the sim. Comes with Logbook. HangarEvery add-on in your Community folder, found. Comes with Logbook. SquawkvPilot on your iPhone or iPad: ATC messages, a live map, alerts. RampParked airliners at the empty gates, in the right airlines. Vantage Coming soonCameras, sound and feel.
