A vPilot plugin and an iPhone and iPad app: ATC messages, the connection, a live map and alerts.

**New in 6.7:** the route line stays on the map when you zoom in at either end of a route over the North Pole or across the date line.

**Most pilots don't need this file directly:** download Flight Deck Manager from the website, and it installs and updates this app for you. If Squawk is already installed through the Manager, close vPilot and select **Update** on Squawk's card.
